# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Revenue from operations was 1,248 crore in Q1 FY27, up 18.0% year-on-year. [S3]
- Profit after tax was 82 crore in Q1 FY27, up 5.9% year-on-year. [S3]
- Promoters and the promoter group held 58.2% of the company as of 30 June 2026. [S2]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- Management said the new Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will contribute from the second half. [S3] [S5]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- The Managing Director said data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue and thinks that can double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- The company disclosed on 2 September 2026 that it received a GST demand order for 46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, which the CFO attributed mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, which the Managing Director stated was mostly due to copper price increases. [S3] [S5]

## Open questions

- A news article reports Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,428 crore, while the company's press release reports 1,248 crore. What explains this discrepancy? [S3] [S4]
- The Managing Director stated that about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a lag of one to two quarters. How will this lag affect margins in the next two quarters? [S5]
- The company intends to file an appeal against the GST demand order. What is the expected timeline and outcome of this appeal? [S8]
