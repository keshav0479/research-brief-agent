# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Revenue from operations for Q1 FY27 was 1,248 crore rupees, an 18.0% increase year-over-year. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was 82 crore rupees, a 5.9% increase year-over-year. [S3]
- Promoters and the promoter group held 58.2% of the company's shares as of June 2026. [S2]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore rupees a year earlier. [S3]
- Management states the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will contribute from the second half of the year. [S3] [S5]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, and management expects data-centre cables, currently about 9% of domestic revenue, to double in two years. [S3] [S5]
- The share of promoter holdings pledged with lenders decreased to 4.1% as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025. [S2]

## Bear case

- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, a change the CFO attributed mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, which the Managing Director stated was mostly due to a sharp rise in copper prices. [S3] [S5]
- Management states that about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a lag of one to two quarters. [S5]

## Open questions

- The company's official press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of 1,248 crore rupees, while a news article reports 1,428 crore rupees. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figure? [S3] [S4]
- The company received a 46.3 crore rupee GST demand, intends to appeal it, and states it does not expect a material impact on its financial position. What is the timeline for this appeal and what are the potential financial implications if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S8]
- The CFO stated that the company is monitoring increased receivable days closely and has not seen any defaults. What specific measures are in place to manage the payment delays from the two large state utility customers? [S5]
