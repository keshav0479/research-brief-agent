# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of 1,248 crore rupees, EBITDA of 140 crore rupees, and an EBITDA margin of 11.2%. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was 82 crore rupees, a 5.9% increase from the previous year. [S3]
- As of June 2026, 4.1% of the promoter holding was pledged, down from 11.5% in June 2025. [S2]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore rupees a year ago. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- Management maintains its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. [S5]
- Management believes that data-centre cables, currently about 9 percent of domestic revenue, can double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- The Chief Financial Officer stated on the earnings call that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]

## Open questions

- The company press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,248 crore rupees, while a news article reports 1,428 crore rupees. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
