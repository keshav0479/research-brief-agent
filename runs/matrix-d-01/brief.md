# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors listed on the NSE under the ticker SRVCABLE. [S3]
- For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore and profit after tax of ₹82 crore, representing year-on-year growth of 18.0% and 5.9% respectively. [S3]
- As of June 2026, promoters held 58.2% of the company, with 4.1% of that holding pledged, a significant decrease from 11.5% pledged in June 2025. [S2]

## Bull case

- The new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, with management noting it will contribute from the second half. [S3] [S5]

## Bear case

- EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, down 170 basis points from 12.9% in the prior year quarter, primarily due to rising copper costs. [S3] [S4]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, a trend the CFO attributes to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
