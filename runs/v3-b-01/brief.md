# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors listed on the NSE. [S3]
- Consolidated profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was ₹82 crore, a 5.9% increase from ₹77 crore in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- As of June 2026, promoters and the promoter group held 58.2% of the company's shares. [S2]
- The share of promoter holdings pledged with lenders was 4.1% as of June 2026, down from 11.5% a year earlier. [S2]

## Bull case

- Consolidated revenue from operations for Q1 FY27 was ₹1,248 crore, an 18.0% increase year-on-year. [S3]
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. [S3]
- Management states that the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
- The Managing Director noted that data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue and expects this share to double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- Consolidated EBITDA margin for Q1 FY27 was 11.2%, down 170 basis points from 12.9% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- The CFO stated that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- The company disclosed that it received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, relating to input tax credit claims for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
- The company disclosed a ₹46.3 crore GST demand, intends to appeal it, and states it does not expect a material impact on its financial position. What is the current status of this appeal and the final outcome? [S8]
- Management attributes the margin decline mostly to copper prices and notes that price-variation clauses reset with a lag of one to two quarters. How much of the margin contraction is specifically due to competitive pricing versus raw material costs? [S5]
