# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore and profit after tax of ₹82 crore. [S3]
- As of 30 June 2026, promoters held 58.2% of the company, with 4.1% of that holding pledged. [S2]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in the same period last year. [S3]

## Bull case

- Revenue from operations rose 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore in Q1 FY27. [S3]
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- The company says the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and contribute from the second half. [S3] [S5]
- Management thinks data-centre cables, currently about 9% of domestic revenue, can double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- The company disclosed on 2 September 2026 that it received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, for input tax credits claimed in FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, a change the CFO attributed mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, which the Managing Director stated was mostly due to copper price increases. [S3] [S5]
- Management stated that about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a lag of one to two quarters. [S5]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
- The company disclosed a ₹46.3 crore GST demand, intends to appeal it, and does not expect a material impact on its financial position. What is the timeline for this appeal and what are the potential financial implications if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S8]
- The CFO stated that receivable days increased to 96 but that no defaults have been seen. What specific steps is the company taking to manage the payment delays from the two large state utility customers? [S5]
