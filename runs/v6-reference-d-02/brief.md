# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore and profit after tax of ₹82 crore. [S3]
- As of 30 June 2026, promoters held 58.2% of the company, with 4.1% of that holding pledged, down from 11.5% a year earlier. [S2]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in the same quarter of the previous year. [S3]
- The company says the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will contribute from the second half. [S3] [S5]
- Management said data-centre cables are about 9 percent of domestic revenue and thinks that share can double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- The company disclosed on 2 September 2026 that it received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, related to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, a change the CFO attributed mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, which the Managing Director stated was mostly due to copper price increases. [S3] [S5]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
- The company disclosed a ₹46.3 crore GST demand and stated it intends to appeal, while not expecting a material impact on its financial position. What is the current status of this appeal and the potential financial exposure if the demand is upheld? [S8]
