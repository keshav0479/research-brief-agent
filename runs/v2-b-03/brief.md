# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors listed on the NSE. [S3]
- For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore and profit after tax of ₹82 crore. [S3]
- As of June 2026, promoters held 58.2% of the company, with 4.1% of that holding pledged, down from 11.5% in June 2025. [S2]
- Institutional ownership increased in June 2026, with FIIs holding 11.8% and DIIs holding 14.6% of the shares. [S2]

## Bull case

- Revenue from operations grew 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore in Q1 FY27. [S3]
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- Management states the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in the same period last year. [S3]

## Bear case

- EBITDA margin declined by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, a contraction attributed to a sharp rise in copper prices during the quarter. [S3] [S4]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- The company received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, related to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]
- Management notes that price-variation clauses in about two-thirds of contracts reset with a lag of one to two quarters, which may delay full cost pass-through. [S5]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
- Management intends to appeal the ₹46.3 crore GST demand and states it does not expect a material impact. What is the timeline and potential outcome of this appeal? [S8]
- Management believes data-centre cables, currently 9% of domestic revenue, can double in two years. What specific market conditions or contracts support this growth expectation? [S5]
