# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of 1,248 crore rupees, EBITDA of 140 crore rupees, an EBITDA margin of 11.2%, and profit after tax of 82 crore rupees. [S3]
- As of June 2026, promoters held 58.2% of the company, with 4.1% of that holding pledged. [S2]

## Bull case

- The company reported that its order book stood at 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore rupees a year ago. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- Management maintains its revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent for the full year FY27. [S5]
- The company says the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]

## Bear case

- The CFO stated on the earnings call that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- The company disclosed that it received a GST demand order for 46.3 crore rupees, including interest and penalty, relating to input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,248 crore rupees, while a news article reports 1,428 crore rupees. What explains this discrepancy between the company's official filing and the secondary news report? [S3] [S4]
- A March 2024 news article reported promoter pledge at 35%, while the June 2026 exchange filing shows pledged shares at 4.1% of promoter holding. What explains the difference between the 2024 news report and the 2026 official filing? [S1] [S2]
- Management states that about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a lag of one to two quarters. How will this lag affect margins in the upcoming quarters? [S5]
