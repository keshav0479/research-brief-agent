# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- The EBITDA margin for Q1 FY27 was 11.2%, down 170 basis points from 12.9% in the same quarter last year. [S3]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore rupees as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore rupees a year ago. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- Management maintains a full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. [S5]
- The CFO reported that net debt to equity is 0.35 times. [S5]
- The company says the new Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]

## Bear case

- The company's official press release reports revenue from operations of 1,248 crore rupees, an 18.0% increase year-over-year. [S3]
- The company's press release reports that the EBITDA margin was 11.2% in Q1 FY27, down 170 basis points from 12.9% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- The CFO stated that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- The company disclosed that it received a GST demand order for 46.3 crore rupees relating to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]

## Open questions

- The company release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of 1,248 crore rupees, while a news report cites 1,428 crore rupees. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figures? [S3] [S4]
- A March 2024 news article reported promoter pledging at 35%, while the June 2026 exchange filing shows pledging at 4.1% of promoter holding. What explains the difference between these reported pledge levels? [S1] [S2]
- The company disclosed a 46.3 crore rupee GST demand and stated it intends to appeal and does not expect a material impact. What is the outcome of the intended appeal? [S8]
