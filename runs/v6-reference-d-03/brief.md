# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Revenue from operations for Q1 FY27 was 1,248 crore, an 18.0% increase year-over-year. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was 82 crore, a 5.9% increase year-over-year. [S3]
- Promoters and the promoter group held 58.2% of the company as of June 2026. [S2]

## Bull case

- The order book stood at 3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from 3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- The new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
- The Managing Director said data-centre cables are now about 9% of domestic revenue and that he thinks that can double in two years. [S5]

## Bear case

- The company disclosed on 2 September 2026 that it received a GST demand order for 46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, related to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]
- The CFO stated that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, attributing the rise mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, which the Managing Director said was affected by a sharp rise in copper prices during the quarter. [S3]
- The Managing Director said that about two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, but these reset with a lag of one to two quarters. [S5]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of 1,248 crore, while a news article reports 1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy in the reported revenue figure? [S3] [S4]
- The company disclosed a 46.3 crore GST demand and says it intends to file an appeal, while also stating it does not expect a material impact on its financial position. What is the timeline for this appeal and the potential financial exposure if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S8]
- The CFO stated that receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to two large state utility customers, and that the company is monitoring this closely without seeing any defaults. What specific steps are being taken to mitigate the risk of payment delays from these customers? [S5]
