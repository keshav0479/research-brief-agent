# SRVCABLE research brief

## Snapshot

- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors listed on the NSE. [S3]

## Bull case

- Revenue from operations increased 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore in Q1 FY27. [S3]
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. [S3]
- Management states the new Bharuch plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in the same quarter of the previous year. [S3]

## Bear case

- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, down from 12.9% in the prior year period. [S3] [S4]
- Management attributes the increase in receivable days from 78 to 96 over the last year mainly to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S5]
- The company received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, relating to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S8]

## Open questions

- The company's press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore. What explains this discrepancy between the official filing and the secondary report? [S3] [S4]
